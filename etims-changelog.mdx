Changes to the `/v1` endpoints, and to the way they are documented, are listed here. A change that could break an
existing integration is marked **breaking**.

<Update label="2026-10-03" description="Initial publication">
  The `/v1` surface is documented for the first time:

  - **Items**: create, update, read and register items with KRA.
  - **Sales**: issue sales, read them, list them, download the receipt as a PDF (with COPY receipts), and retry.
  - **Credit notes**: full reversal of an accepted sale.
  - **Stock**: adjustments and transfers between branches.
  - **Purchases**: pull supplier invoices from KRA, then list and read them (`purchases:read`).
  - **Lookups**: KRA code lists and item classifications.
  - **Businesses**: list businesses and branches.
  - **Result callbacks**: `callbackUrl` on sales and credit notes, with retries and a resend call. Callbacks are not
    signed.
  - **Idempotency** on sales and credit notes with the `Idempotency-Key` header.
  - **Rate limits**: 120 requests per minute per application by default, with `X-RateLimit-*` headers.
</Update>
