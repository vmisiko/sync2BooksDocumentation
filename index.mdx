Sync2Books is two products behind one API key: an **Accounting API** that syncs financial
data to accounting systems (QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage, Dynamics 365 Business Central, Odoo),
and a **Compliance API** that keeps your Kenyan customers tax-compliant with KRA eTIMS —
without building against each system yourself.
